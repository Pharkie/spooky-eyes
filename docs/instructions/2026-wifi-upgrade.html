1Switch Spooky Eyes off.
2Connect the wires. Connect each wire to the new ESP32-C3.
3Power on. Switch Spooky Eyes on: the eyes start their show.
4Connect to your WiFi (optional). On a phone, tablet or laptop, join the WiFi Spooky Eyes makes:
Network
Spooky Pumpkin
Password
spookyeyes
Setup page
pumpkin.local or 192.168.4.1
After a few seconds, a setup page should open (if not, tap Sign in or open the setup page above): name your pumpkin, pick your WiFi (2.4 GHz), enter your WiFi password and tap Connect.
5Enjoy. Open http://pumpkin.local on your phone (if that doesn’t work, type the numbers the eyes scroll instead).
Then try the buttons, design your own show and make Spooky Eyes talk!

Setup page won’t open? Forget Spooky Pumpkin in your phone’s WiFi settings, switch Spooky Eyes off and on, then join again (switching mobile data off can help).

Spooky Pumpkin WiFi gone? Unused for 10 minutes, it switches off to save batteries: press BOOT.

Page won’t open later? Check your phone is on your WiFi, or switch Spooky Eyes off and on.

On the doorstep? Out of reach of your WiFi for 3 minutes, Spooky Eyes brings back the Spooky Pumpkin WiFi: join and open pumpkin.local. Back in range, Spooky Eyes rejoins your WiFi.

BOOT button (on the board): press to repeat the address; hold 5 seconds to forget your WiFi.

No WiFi to hand? On the setup page, “just use the buttons” works on Spooky Eyes’ own WiFi.
